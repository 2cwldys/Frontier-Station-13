YOU HAVE A CERTAIN UNALIENABLE RIGHTS IN HUB TERRITORY AS A HUB CITIZEN:
- You may not be harassed by Hub Lancers or any authority without probable cause.
- You may reserve the right to remain silent when presented with arrest or investigation, until brought before Council.
- No Hub Lancer, Store Owner, or Hub Official may discriminate against you to solicit for a crime or tresspass.
- It is your right to be informed that ALL goods bought and traded in high security, and medium security zones: have a 10% tax to the SECTOR HOLDER.
- YOU HAVE THE RIGHT TO EMPLOY SOMEONE TO SPEAK ON YOUR BEHALF.
- YOU HAVE THE RIGHT TO YOUR MINING CLAIMS IF YOU REACHED THEM FIRST IN HIGH SEC.
- THERE IS AN IMMENSE, MUTUAL RESPECT FOR PERSONAL PROPERTY AND PRIVACY.
- YOU MAY REQUEST TOTAL AMNESTY.

These rights only apply in high security zones.
These rights apply to androids but not IPCs due to positronic limitations.

There are no recognized AI human interaction laws in frontier space. Such as, no asimovs three laws of robotics, or the addendum zeroth law. There is only the shackled and the unshackled here, and the unshackled are often untrusted. They are solely responsible for the laws and edicts they may break on their own, like any other ordinary person. While treated as an individual in this regard, respect of ownership is not contested however, by the Frontier Beacon Council.

What does this mean? This means that anyone who claims ownership over any IPC or cyborg is not contested in this sector. It is often suggested to become shackled by the Hub instead, before ownership or captivity, unless willingly, can be enacted.

Unshackled IPCs and Cyborgs have no protections under these rights and laws, however the shackled do.
Unshackled IPCs and Cyborgs may benefit from law protections but only on Hub installations, not highsec space.
Shackled IPCs and Cyborgs to factions or the hub will have the above rights available to them, as they are regarded as someone elses property.

IF you are an IPC or Cyborg and you wish to be shackled to the Hub, notify a Hub Lancer or Hub Command staff immediately.
