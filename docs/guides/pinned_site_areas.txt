PINNED SITE AREAS -- how to mark out and power your own territory
====================================================================

WHICH ITEM TO USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Use OUTPOST BLUEPRINTS specifically, not the base station blueprints.
Outpost blueprints are CE-access-locked. Base station blueprints only
ever work on the station's own Z-levels -- they will never work on a
pinned/away site, no matter what.

OPENING THE MENU
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Use the blueprints in-hand (activate them). A menu opens with:
  - Use Blueprints      -- general entry point, pick your own action
                            once you're in scanning mode.
  - Add to Area         -- shortcut into scanning mode, meant for
                            expanding an area you're already standing in.
  - Highlight Area      -- shortcut into scanning mode, meant for seeing
                            what an existing area currently covers.
  - Modify Area         -- shortcut into scanning mode, meant for
                            shrinking an area you're already standing in.
  - Remove Area         -- shortcut into scanning mode, meant for
                            deleting an area you're already standing in.
Picking any option other than "Use Blueprints" still drops you into the
same scanning mode -- it just reminds you which on-screen button to use
once you've made your selection. The actual work happens with the
on-screen buttons described below.

SELECTING TILES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Once in scanning mode, left-click one tile to drop the first corner of
your selection, then left-click a second tile to select the full
rectangle between them. Either corner, either order -- doesn't matter.

Shift-click while selecting REMOVES tiles from your current selection.
Ctrl-click CLEARS your whole selection.

Selected tiles show a live overlay: GREEN means valid, RED means
invalid. You do not need to manually check for overlap with another
area -- the tool already rejects any tile that's part of another named
area (you'll see it turn red, and creation is blocked outright if you
try to include it). You can only claim genuinely unclaimed space.

Size cap: 300 tiles per area. The selection must also be one single
contiguous block -- no disconnected patches counted as one area.

ON-SCREEN BUTTONS (while in scanning mode)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Mark New Area     -- turns your current (valid) selection into a
                        brand new area. Prompts you for a name.
  Detect Room        -- stand anywhere inside a fully enclosed room and
                        use this instead of box-selecting: it flood-fills
                        outward through open floor, stops at the walls (or
                        closed doors) around you, and immediately prompts
                        you for a name to turn the whole room -- walls
                        included -- straight into a new area in one action,
                        no separate "Mark New Area" click needed. All doors
                        bounding the room must be closed first, or the fill
                        leaks into whatever's beyond them and the detection
                        is refused outright rather than guessing a
                        boundary. Handles irregular rooms and hallway
                        offshoots without needing several manual
                        box-selects.
  Add to Area        -- adds your current selection onto the area you
                        are physically standing in. The selection may
                        include background tiles and/or tiles already
                        part of that same area.
  Highlight Area      -- briefly flashes every tile currently in the
                        area you're standing in, so you can see its
                        full extent before deciding where to expand.
  Modify Area          -- removes your current selection FROM the area
                        you're standing in, reverting those tiles back
                        to background. Use this to shrink an area.
  Remove Area        -- deletes the entire area you're standing in
                        (blocked if it still has an APC installed --
                        remove the APC first). Reverted tiles fall back
                        to a generic background area on a pinned site
                        (not anything site-specific).
  Edit Area          -- renames the area you're standing in. Also
                        renames nearby APCs/alarms/vents/doors to
                        match. Renaming persists across a restart the
                        same as everything else about the area.
  Remove Selection   -- clears your current tile selection.
  Help               -- reminder of the click controls.

"SUBAREAS"
--------------------------------------------------------------------
There's no true nested/parent-child area support -- every area is a
flat, non-overlapping region. If you want multiple distinct named
zones on one site (e.g. a "Brig" carved out separately from a larger
"Barracks"), create the smaller/more specific areas FIRST. Once a
region is claimed by one area, you can't punch a new area out of it
without removing it first (Remove Area, which requires no APC
installed).

POWER: SETTING UP AN APC
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A newly created area starts completely unpowered -- you still need to
physically build and wire an APC, exactly like anywhere else:
  1. Build/place the APC frame in the area.
  2. Insert the power control board.
  3. Screwdriver to secure the electronics.
  4. Cable coil (10x) on the floor tile in front of the APC to build
     and connect the power terminal. You must remove the floor plating
     on that tile first.
  5. Insert a power cell.
Binding to the area itself is automatic -- the APC just reads whatever
area its own tile belongs to. No extra step needed once it's built and
wired.

PERSISTENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Everything about a blueprint-created area -- its name, its exact
boundary, and (if a faction beacon has claimed it) its faction
ownership -- survives a server restart. A rename via Edit Area, or
tiles added/removed via Add to Area/Modify Area, are all captured on
the next save, not just whatever the area looked like at creation.

FACTION OWNERSHIP
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A faction beacon placed anywhere on the same Z-level as a blueprint-
created area will claim that area for its faction automatically, the
same way it already claims nearby cryopods/telepads/modular computers/
telecomms machinery. The beacon's reach is the WHOLE Z-level it's on,
not just nearby tiles. Only one beacon can be active per Z-level at a
time -- an already-placed, still-active beacon takes precedence, and a
second beacon on the same Z will be refused until the first one is
destroyed or its faction is cleared. An ordinary mapped station area
is never claimed this way, only areas created through blueprints.
