====================================================================
 PIRACY BEACONS AND FACTION BEACONS
====================================================================

This document covers two separate beacon systems: the piracy beacon
(unlocks black-market cargo access in nullsec) and the faction beacon
(claims a Z-level of territory for a faction). They are unrelated to
each other and unrelated to a player-built docking beacon (see
playerbuiltshuttles.txt for that).


--------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. PIRACY BEACON
--------------------------------------------------------------------

A piracy beacon is a crude, unlicensed anchor beacon. It never claims
territory, never grants security bonuses, and never sweeps or tags
anything to a faction. Its only job is to mark a Z-level as having
"piracy infrastructure," which unlocks black-market options on cargo
consoles located on that same Z-level.

WHERE IT WORKS
  A piracy beacon only ever functions in genuine unregulated
  (nullsec) space. It will refuse to assemble at all in highsec, and
  it stays dormant (powered but inactive) if built in medsec/highsec
  or on a Z-level a live mission is currently using. Moving into
  regulated space, or a mission claiming the sector, will silently
  put an already-built beacon back to sleep until conditions are
  right again -- you don't need to rebuild it, just wait or relocate.

POWERING IT ON
  Click the beacon to toggle it on/off. It needs fuel to actually
  stay running (see below) -- if you try to power it on with no
  fuel, it will refuse.

FUEL
  Feed it spacecash (bills or bundles, not an e-wallet) by clicking
  the beacon with the cash in hand. It has a maximum reserve. While
  powered and actually operational (in nullsec, not dormant), it
  slowly drains that reserve over real time and automatically shuts
  itself off once it runs dry. Alt-click the beacon to withdraw
  stored credits back out as a cash bundle.

GETTING THE SYNDICATE UPLINK TERMINAL
  Once a piracy beacon is powered and operational on a Z-level:
    1. You need a Cargo Order console that is faction-shackled
       (linked to a faction's own network) and physically located on
       that same Z-level.
    2. Open the Cargo Order program and browse to the Operations
       category. A "Syndicate Uplink Terminal" item will now be
       visible in the catalog -- it is hidden entirely unless both
       conditions above are met.
    3. Order it like any other crate. It costs 250,000 credits and
       contains a modified radio that conceals a black-market
       purchasing terminal (a standard syndicate uplink).
  A personal (non-faction-shackled) console can never order this,
  regardless of beacon state -- it specifically requires a faction
  network link.

SELLING ILLEGAL GOODS
  The Cargo Exports program's sell catalog normally hides anything
  tagged as illegal contraband. While a powered, operational piracy
  beacon sits on the same Z-level as the exporting console, those
  illegal items become sellable through it -- a fencing channel for
  stolen or black-market goods that isn't available anywhere else.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. FACTION BEACON
--------------------------------------------------------------------

A faction beacon claims an entire Z-level (and every connected deck
of the same station/site) as one faction's own territory. Placement
of the beacon itself is an admin/mapping decision -- it isn't
something a faction can build wherever they like -- but once one
exists, configuring and running it is normal faction business.

WHAT CLAIMING A Z-LEVEL DOES
  Once a beacon successfully claims its Z-level(s):
    - Every compatible piece of infrastructure on that Z that isn't
      already tagged to a different faction (cryopods, telepads,
      consoles, telecomms, and more) gets automatically swept and
      tagged to the beacon's faction over time -- you don't need to
      tag every single machine by hand.
    - The Z-level's security tier is raised to at least medsec (or
      highsec, for the special Hub beacon variant). A configurable
      "security radius" also raises nearby overmap sectors around it
      to the same minimum tier.
    - The Z-level is added to the persistence save list, so
      everything built there survives a server restart.
    - Non-members can be blocked from entering the territory,
      subject to the server-wide faction raiding toggle (see below).
  Destroying the beacon by combat damage reverses all of this: the
  Z-level stops being saved and drops back to nullsec. Admin
  deletion does not trigger this reversal.

POWERING IT ON
  The beacon starts locked and unpowered. Alt-click to unlock it
  (like an APC or air alarm) -- while locked, only admins can touch
  its power state. Once unlocked, click it to open its interface and
  use the power toggle. Turning it on requires command rank in the
  faction it's configured for (see Faction Tagger below); an
  unconfigured beacon can't be powered on at all.

FUEL
  Same system as the piracy beacon -- feed it spacecash by clicking
  it with cash in hand, alt-click to withdraw. It drains slowly while
  powered and running, and auto-shuts-off if it runs out.

ASSIGNING A FACTION -- THE FACTION TAGGER
  A beacon has no faction until someone configures one. This is done
  with a Faction Tagger item, not through the beacon's own interface
  directly:
    1. Point-and-click the faction tagger at the beacon (or at any
       other compatible machine -- consoles, cryopods, telecomms,
       turrets, doors, and more all use the same tool).
    2. Its window shows the target's current faction (if any). Pick
       "Set" and choose a faction from the dropdown to claim it, or
       "Release" to clear an existing tag.
    3. Setting a faction requires officer-level access in that
       faction. Retagging something already claimed by a DIFFERENT
       faction requires officer access in that faction too, not just
       the one you're trying to switch to.
  Small business/company-tier factions cannot claim a beacon at all
  -- they have no pinned site of their own and must operate out of a
  full faction's territory instead.

PUBLIC TERRITORY
  From the beacon's own interface, faction command can toggle
  "Public Territory." A public territory always allows non-members
  to enter, even while the server-wide faction raiding toggle is
  turned off (which normally blocks outsiders from every claimed
  territory at once). A private (non-public) territory is subject to
  that raiding toggle like any other claimed site.

SECURITY RADIUS
  An admin-adjustable setting controlling how many overmap sectors
  out (beyond the beacon's own Z) also get bumped to the beacon's
  minimum security tier. A radius of 0 means only the beacon's own
  Z-level is affected.

RENAMING THE SITE
  Faction command can also rename the overmap display name of the
  beacon's own site from its interface -- this persists across
  restarts. Leaving the new name blank restores the default.
